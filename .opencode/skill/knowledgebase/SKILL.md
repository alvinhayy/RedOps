Use local niche directories and the read-only RAG MCP to ground recommendations. Keep
source URLs, document paths, headings, and evidence references in handoffs. Prefer the
narrowest niche matching the observed service or artifact, and label uncertainty.

The book-derived `knowledge/api/` archive is intentionally excluded from the active
corpus. Do not traverse or cite it unless the operator explicitly changes that policy.
Never put credentials, tokens, cookies, raw identity exports, or target loot in the
knowledge tree.
