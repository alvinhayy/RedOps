name: redops-orchestration
description: Route OpenCode work through the RedOps specialist graph and source-grounded RAG.
---

Use native OpenCode delegation and Herdr tabs. Retrieve citations with the read-only RAG
MCP or configured local references, then preserve the handoff contract in
`engagements/<name>/notes.md`. The role graph is defined in `.opencode/agent/` and
`agents/agent-graph.yaml`; capability/MCP policy remains in `agents/registry.yaml`.
The RedOps CLI may be used for maintenance or offline troubleshooting, but it is not a
runtime prerequisite for the OpenCode orchestrator.
